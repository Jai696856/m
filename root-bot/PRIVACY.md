# Privacy Policy: Claude Fan Art Bot

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Claude Fan Art Bot is a community-made bot for the Claude Fan Art community on Root.
It is a fan project and is not affiliated with Anthropic.

## What the bot stores

For each member who chats in a community where the bot is installed, it stores:

- your Root user ID
- your community nickname (so the leaderboard can show your name)
- your XP total and the time you last earned XP

It also stores one setting per community: the channel chosen for welcome messages.

## What the bot does not store

- It does not save the text of your messages. It only reads a message to check whether it is a command and to award XP.
- It does not store email addresses, passwords, files or images.

## Why

The data is used only to run the bot's features: levels, the leaderboard, level-up roles and welcome messages.

## Where it is kept and who can see it

Data is kept in the bot's own storage on Root's servers. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.
Your nickname, level and XP can be seen by other members through `/level` and `/leaderboard`.

## How long it is kept

XP data is kept while the bot is installed in the community. If the bot is removed, its data is no longer used.

## Deleting your data

To have your XP and nickname deleted, ask the owner of the Claude Fan Art community on Root,
or open an issue at https://github.com/Jai696856/m/issues. Requests are handled within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
