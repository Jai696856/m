# Privacy Policy: Claude Fan Art Bot

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Claude Fan Art Bot is a community-made bot for the Claude Fan Art community on Root.
It is a fan project and is not affiliated with Anthropic.

## What the bot stores

For members of a community where the bot is installed, it may store:

- **Levels:** your Root user ID, community nickname, XP total, the time you last earned XP, how many messages with images you posted, and your `/daily` streak
- **Moderation:** warnings given to you by moderators (the reason, which moderator gave it, and when)
- **Tickets:** while a ticket is open, who opened it, its topic and when it was opened
- **Reminders:** the text of a reminder you set with `/remind`, until it is delivered
- **Art trades:** your name and optional note while you wait in the `/trade` queue (removed after a match, or after 7 days)
- **Giveaways:** the user IDs of people who entered and who won

It also stores community settings: the welcome, mod-log and ticket channels.

## What the bot does not store

- It does not save the text of ordinary messages. It only reads a message to check whether it is a command, whether it has an image attached, and to award XP.
- It does not store email addresses, passwords, files or images.

## Why

The data is used only to run the bot's features: levels, the leaderboard, level roles, welcomes, giveaways, reminders, art trades, tickets and moderation.

## Where it is kept and who can see it

Data is kept in the bot's own storage on Root's servers. It is not sold or shared with anyone outside the community.

- Other members can see your nickname, level, rank, XP, art post count and streak through `/level`, `/profile` and `/leaderboard`.
- Moderators can see warnings with `/warnings`. Moderation actions and ticket summaries are posted in the community's private mod-log channel, if one is set.

## How long it is kept

- Level data and warnings are kept while the bot is installed, until a moderator clears them or you ask for deletion.
- Reminders are deleted when delivered. Ticket records are deleted when the ticket is closed. Trade queue entries expire after 7 days.
- If the bot is removed from a community, its data for that community is no longer used.

## Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, ask the owner of the Claude Fan Art community on Root, or open an issue at
https://github.com/Jai696856/m/issues. Requests are handled within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
